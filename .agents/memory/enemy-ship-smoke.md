---
name: Enemy ship smoke
description: The visual exhaust treatment for the enemy ship.
---

Only the enemy ship emits the decorative smoke trail. It should look like short-lived soft neon puffs that disperse behind its movement direction, not like a hard Tron ribbon and not like a player effect.

**Why:** The player explicitly corrected that the trail belongs to the enemy ship and should read as smoke.

**How to apply:** Keep the smoke purely visual, low-opacity, short-lived, and excluded from all collision, score, and movement logic.
