---
name: Approved enemy art
description: The user-approved Fragments Neon enemy concept board and the rule for preserving it.
---

The four enemy designs in the approved concept board are the source of truth for Fragments Neon: preserve their silhouettes, colors, glow treatment, and pixel character. Animation should be applied as motion, scale, sway, or rotation around the extracted artwork, not by generating replacement creatures or recoloring them.

**Why:** Replacing the validated board with visually similar generated assets was rejected because “similar” is not acceptable for these enemies.

**How to apply:** When changing enemy visuals, start from the approved concept-board asset already present in the workspace and only add animation/rendering behavior. Do not call image generation to redesign these four enemies.
