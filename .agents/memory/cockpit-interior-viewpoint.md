---
name: Cockpit interior viewpoint
description: The Fragments Neon cockpit HUD direction is an interior pilot-seat view rather than an exterior spacecraft illustration.
---

All future cockpit concepts should read immediately as a pilot-facing interior: front canopy or windshield, structural ribs, dashboard depth, physical controls, instrument bays, and an integrated crystal. Avoid wings, engines, noses, or full external hull silhouettes as the primary form.

**Why:** Exterior ship artwork was repeatedly mistaken for a spacecraft sprite instead of a cockpit HUD. The interior perspective communicates the intended HUD role and supports the telemetry counters.

**How to apply:** Start every new cockpit variation from the pilot-seat viewpoint and place SCORE, SECTEUR, BOUCLIERS, and ZONE / 80 inside physical dashboard instrumentation.
