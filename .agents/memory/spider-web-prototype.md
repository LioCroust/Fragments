---
name: Spider thread prototype
description: The gameplay and visual rules for the spider's predictive thread shot.
---

The spider's first ability is a fast thread projectile aimed just ahead of the drone's predicted future position. It uses the drone's current direction and speed, has no warning phase, and becomes a full centered web sprite when it reaches the target area. The anchored web slows the drone heavily without dealing damage or consuming a shield.

**Why:** The user preferred a spider that actively reads movement and fires at the correct angle and location, rather than announcing a static trap.

**How to apply:** Keep the projectile fast but dodgeable, keep the full web inside the arena bounds, and increase difficulty later through cooldown, lead accuracy, speed, duration, or multiple shots rather than instant damage.
